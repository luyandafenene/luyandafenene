Professional Profile

An educator committed to meaningful learning.

A concise professional profile built around subject knowledge, learner development, communication and responsible educational leadership.

I am a SACE Registered Educator with a Bachelor of Education in Further Education and Training, majoring in English First Additional Language and History. I graduated from Walter Sisulu University in 2023 and am completing an Honours degree in Education Management.

My teaching experience has developed across different school contexts and subjects, strengthening my ability to adapt lessons to diverse learners, support academic progress and contribute positively to school communities.

I value clear communication, preparation, fairness and a classroom environment where learners are encouraged to participate, think critically and develop confidence.

“Education is not only about what learners know; it is also about who they become through the learning process.”

My professional approach combines sound curriculum knowledge with learner-centred teaching, purposeful assessment and continuous professional growth.
